Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

NTAS subscriptions

  • Free trial: no NTAS fee is charged during the trial. Meta's WhatsApp message charges during the trial are billed by Meta and are not refundable by us.
  • Cancelling: cancel any time by emailing billing@nextechalign.com. Monthly plans stop at the end of the current paid month; you keep access until then. We do not refund part-months.
  • Annual plans: cancel within 14 days of payment for a full refund. After 14 days, the plan runs until the end of the paid year and is not refunded.
  • Setup fee: fully refundable if you cancel before the onboarding session takes place; not refundable after onboarding is done.
  • Meta charges: WhatsApp message charges are billed to you directly by Meta. We cannot refund them; contact Meta for billing questions about them.

IT services and hardware

  • Advance payments for projects are refundable, less costs already incurred (materials ordered, work done), if you cancel before installation starts.
  • Hardware is covered by the manufacturer's warranty. Faulty items reported within 7 days of delivery are replaced or repaired.
  • AMC and support contracts follow the cancellation terms in the signed contract.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.
