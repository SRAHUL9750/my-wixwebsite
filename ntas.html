QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. NTAS connects through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). Your WhatsApp Business Account stays in your own name and under your control. NTAS is a product of NexTechAlign Systems and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta or WhatsApp.

Who pays for WhatsApp messages?

Meta charges per delivered template message (for example, marketing and utility messages) and bills your card directly. Replies to customers within 24 hours of their last message are free. We show you Meta's current rate before your first campaign.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?

Usually yes. The number either needs to be new, or removed from the regular WhatsApp app before connecting. Meta also supports connecting some WhatsApp Business app numbers; we'll check which option suits you during the demo.

What do I need for setup?

A Facebook login of a business admin, the phone number you want to use, a business document (for example GST certificate), and a card to add in WhatsApp Manager for Meta's charges.

Can I message any number?

No. WhatsApp requires that customers agree to receive messages from you (opt-in), and every marketing message lets them opt out. This protects your number's quality rating and keeps your account in good standing.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy.
